About Me

Albert Einstein was a German-born theoretical physicist who is best known for developing the special and general theories of relativity. He was born in 1879 and he is one of the most influential scientists in history. His work revolutionized our understanding of time, space and energy.

Greatest Achievements & Awards

  • Developed the Theory of Special Relativity (1905)
  • Published the Theory of General Relativity (1915)
  • Won the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics (awarded in 1922)
  • Received the Copley Medal (1925)
  • Received the Max Planck Medal (1929)
